Single Car Cover - Frequently Asked Questions

What is third party and comprehensive Cover?
Third party cover protects you against claims made by other parties for damage or injury you cause. Comprehensive cover includes third party protection plus coverage for damage to your own vehicle, theft, and other incidents.
What we cover?
We cover vehicle damage, third party liability, theft, fire, and accident-related incidents while you're operating as a rideshare or delivery driver. Coverage includes both on-duty and off-duty periods.
How do I cancel my policy?
You can cancel your policy at any time by contacting our support team via phone or email. Cancellations are processed within 24-48 hours, and you may be eligible for a pro-rated refund depending on your policy terms.
How can I access my quote?
Access your quote by logging into your SharedFare account dashboard or by clicking the link in the email we sent you. You can review, modify, and purchase your coverage directly from your quote page.

Fleet Cover - Frequently Asked Questions

1. What types of vehicles can be included in a fleet policy?
Fleet policies can include sedans, SUVs, vans, and light commercial vehicles used for rideshare or delivery services. All vehicles must meet our eligibility requirements including age, condition, and safety standards.
2. How many vehicles do I need to qualify for fleet Cover?
You need a minimum of 3 vehicles to qualify for our fleet coverage plan. We offer scalable solutions for fleets ranging from 3 to 250+ vehicles.
3. Can I add or remove vehicles during the policy term?
Yes, you can add or remove vehicles at any time during your policy term. Changes are processed within 24 hours, and your premium will be adjusted accordingly on a pro-rated basis.
4. How are premiums calculated for fleet Cover?
Fleet premiums are calculated based on the number of vehicles, vehicle types, driver history, coverage level, and claims history. Bulk pricing discounts apply for larger fleets.
